About

An established Canberra builder.

Summerset Constructions is a Fyshwick-based building and carpentry company working across the ACT and nearby NSW. We started in carpentry and grew into full residential and commercial construction — which means the hands-on craft that got us started still runs through everything we build.

We take on renovations, rebuilds, maintenance and design-led commercial fitouts, and we’re proud to have helped shape a number of Canberra’s homes and hospitality spaces.

Based in Fyshwick, we work across Canberra and the wider ACT, plus Queanbeyan and the surrounding NSW region.
